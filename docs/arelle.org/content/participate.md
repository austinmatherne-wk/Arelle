## How you can help the Arelle project

- **Ask and answer questions** in the [Arelle Users Google
  Group](https://groups.google.com/d/forum/arelle-users)
- [**Contribute
  code**](https://arelle.readthedocs.io/en/latest/contributor_guides/contributing.html)
- Join **our monthly standup** (email <support@arelle.org> for an invite) where
  we discuss upcoming work
